Why we built it

Everyone deserves a coach.

A good coach changes everything. They show you how a lift should look, tell you what to load next, and notice the week you nearly gave up.

They also cost more than most people can spare. So most of us learn alone: copying a stranger across the gym, guessing at the weight, wondering whether an ache means progress or a mistake.

A lot of people stop there. They had the will. What they did not have was someone standing beside them.

Myolith is our way of standing beside you.

Wherever you are starting from, and whatever you can afford, you deserve to be coached.

The name

Myolith

Myo means muscle. Lith means stone.

A body is shaped the way stone is carved: slowly, one small strike after another. No single session changes you. Enough of them will.

Questions

Is Myolith really free?

Yes. The exercise library and the muscle guide are free for good. Your plan, the coach, logging and Progress are part of Pro, and you can try Pro free first.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Your workouts, weight and photos stay on your iPhone.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. All of it, the 3D figure included.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel in Settings, under your Apple Account and Subscriptions. Do it at least a day before the free trial ends and you pay nothing.

I am new to lifting. Is this for me?

Yes. Setup asks how much experience you have. Your first weights start on the light side, and the figure shows you how each move should look.

How is Myolith different from other workout apps?

Every exercise plays on a 3D figure with the working muscles lit, so you can see how to do it. The coach picks your next weight and reps from your own sets. And there is no account, no ads and no need for signal.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Setup asks what equipment you have, so your plan fits it, and Myolith includes home workouts.

Does Myolith work with Apple Watch?

There is no Apple Watch app. If you turn on Health sync, your finished workouts and body weight are saved to Apple Health.
