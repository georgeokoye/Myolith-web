Myolith Pro and billing

What is free and what needs Pro?

The exercise library, with every exercise shown on the 3D figure, and the muscle guide are free. Your training week, plans, guided sessions, workout logging, the coach and everything in Progress are part of Myolith Pro.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Myolith. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

How does the free trial work?

If the yearly plan offers you a free trial, you pay nothing when it starts. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged to your Apple Account. The exact price and terms are shown in the app before you confirm.

I bought Pro but the app is still locked

  • Open the Profile tab and tap Restore purchases under Myolith Pro. The same button is on the upgrade screen.
  • Check that the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you bought with.
  • If a family member or your bank has to approve the purchase, Pro unlocks as soon as it goes through.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Myolith purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves because Apple takes the payment.

Your data

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. Myolith has no accounts and no servers, so your workouts, photos and settings never leave your device unless you export them. This also means we cannot recover data for you.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

  • On the old iPhone, open the Profile tab and choose Export a backup. Save the file to Files or send it to yourself.
  • On the new iPhone, install Myolith and choose I have a backup to restore on the welcome screen, or Import from a backup in the Profile tab.
  • Progress photos are not part of the backup file. An iCloud or computer backup of the whole iPhone includes them.
  • Then tap Restore purchases to unlock Pro again.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything Myolith stored. Workouts and body weight you chose to save to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can remove them under Sharing, Apps, Myolith.

Using the app

Workouts are not showing in Apple Health

Turn on Sync with Apple Health in the Profile tab. If it is on and nothing appears, open the Health app, go to Sharing, Apps, Myolith, and allow it to write workouts and weight.

I am not getting reminders

Turn on Workout reminders in the Profile tab and pick a time. Then check Settings, Notifications, Myolith on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. A Focus mode can also silence them.

The 3D figure is blank or slow

Close the app fully and open it again. If it stays blank, restart the iPhone and make sure iOS is up to date. Myolith needs iOS 17 or later. If the problem continues, email us with your iPhone model.

How do I change my goals, training days or units?

All of these are in the Profile tab and save as you change them: goals, training days, session length, equipment, pounds or kilograms, and your avatar.

The suggested weight is too heavy or too light

Change the weight before you log the set. The first suggestion for a lift is an estimate from your body weight and experience. After your first logged session the coach works from what you actually lifted.

I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?

No. One missed training day puts your streak on hold, and your next session picks it back up. It only resets after two missed training days in a row.
