Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version. Myolith has no accounts and no servers. Your workouts, body weight and photos are saved on your iPhone and are never sent to us. The app has no ads and no tracking.

This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Who we are

Myolith is published by Zitica Trading Inc., a company based in Canada ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach the person in charge of privacy at yuuvenis@protonmail.com.

This policy covers the Myolith app for iPhone and this website.

The Myolith app

Information that stays on your iPhone

The app works without an account. Everything you enter is saved on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your setup answers: name, chosen figure, goal, experience, equipment, training days, session length, focus areas and any areas you asked us to go easy on.
  • Your training history: workouts, sets, weights, reps, times and personal records.
  • Your body weight entries and unit preference.
  • Your progress photos and profile picture.
  • Your own workouts, saved exercises, reminder time and other settings.

We cannot see, read or recover this information. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each of these is optional and is used only on your device. You can change any of them in the Settings app.

  • Camera. To take progress photos and a profile picture.
  • Photos. To choose a picture from your library, and to save a progress image you choose to keep.
  • Apple Health. If you turn on Health sync, the app saves your finished workouts and body weight to Apple Health, and can read your body weight from it. Health information is not sent to us and is never used for advertising.
  • Notifications. For workout reminders, your weekly recap and a reminder before a free trial ends. These are scheduled on your iPhone.

Backups you create

You can export a backup file from the app and import it on another iPhone. You choose where that file goes. We never receive it.

Purchases

Myolith Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, card details or Apple Account email. Apple gives us sales totals and, if you have chosen to share analytics with developers, combined statistics such as crash counts that do not identify you.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools from us or third parties.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

This website

  • No cookies and no analytics. We do not track visits.
  • Preferences in your browser. The site remembers on your own device which figure you picked and whether you have opened the pricing section. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details, as with any web request.
  • Hosting. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and reliability.
  • The App Store. Links to the App Store take you to Apple, where Apple's privacy policy applies.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to deal with your request, and we keep it for as long as that takes and for a reasonable period afterwards.

How we use and share information

We use the limited information described above to answer your messages, to keep the website running and to meet our legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run email and hosting, or where the law requires it.

Our email and hosting providers may store information outside Canada, where it is subject to the laws of that country.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app. You control your data directly: edit or remove entries in the app, turn permissions off in Settings, or delete the app to erase everything.
  • With us. Under Canadian privacy law you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as deletion. Email yuuvenis@protonmail.com and we will respond within the time the law requires.
  • Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local privacy regulator.

Security

Your app data is protected by the security of your iPhone, including its passcode and encryption. Keep your device locked and up to date, and store any backup file you export somewhere safe.

Children

Myolith is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so clearly on this page.
