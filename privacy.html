Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version. Myolith has no accounts and no servers. Your workouts, body weight and photos are saved on your iPhone and are never sent to us. The app has no ads and no tracking.

If you connect Apple Health, that data is used only to run the app on your iPhone. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Who we are

Myolith is published by Zitica Trading Inc., doing business as Thumos HQ, a company based in Canada ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Our privacy officer is the person in charge of protecting personal information, and you can reach them at yuuvenis@protonmail.com.

This policy covers the Myolith app for iPhone and this website.

The Myolith app

Information that stays on your iPhone

The app works without an account. Everything you enter is saved on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your setup answers: name, chosen figure, goal, experience, equipment, training days, session length, focus areas and any areas you asked us to go easy on.
  • Your training history: workouts, sets, weights, reps, times and personal records.
  • Your body weight entries and unit preference.
  • Your progress photos and profile picture.
  • Your own workouts, saved exercises, reminder time and other settings.

We cannot see, read or recover this information.

Apple Health

Health sync is off until you turn it on, and you choose what to allow in Apple's own permission screen. When it is on, Myolith uses Apple Health in these ways only:

  • It writes your finished workouts and the body weight entries you log, so they appear in Apple Health.
  • It reads your body weight, so your entries in Myolith stay up to date.

Health information is used only to provide these features on your iPhone. We never receive it. It is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. It is never sold or shared with anyone. Myolith does not store it in iCloud.

You can stop sharing at any time in the Settings app, under Apps, then Health, then Data Access & Devices. Deleting Myolith does not remove workouts or weights it already wrote to Apple Health. You can delete those in the Health app.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each of these is optional and is used only on your device. You can change any of them in the Settings app.

  • Camera. To take progress photos and a profile picture.
  • Photos. To choose a picture from your library, and to save a progress image you choose to keep.
  • Apple Health. For Health sync, as described above.
  • Notifications. For workout reminders, your weekly recap and a reminder before a free trial ends. These are scheduled on your iPhone.

Pictures from your camera and photo library are never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Internet connections

The app works fully offline. The only time it goes online is for App Store purchases and subscription checks, which are handled by Apple. It does not send your information to us or to any other company.

Backups

  • Backups you export. You can export a backup file from the app and import it on another iPhone. You choose where that file goes. We never receive it.
  • Your iPhone's own backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple includes Myolith's data in that backup under its own terms, so you can restore it on a new iPhone. Data in Apple Health is backed up by Apple Health under your own Apple settings, not by Myolith.

Purchases

Myolith Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, card details or Apple Account email. Apple gives us sales totals and, if you have chosen to share analytics with developers, combined statistics such as crash counts that do not identify you.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools from us or third parties.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

This website

  • No cookies and no analytics. We do not track visits.
  • Preferences in your browser. The site remembers on your own device which figure you picked and whether you have opened the pricing section. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details, as with any web request.
  • Hosting. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and reliability. These logs are kept only for a limited time and then deleted.
  • The App Store. Links to the App Store take you to Apple, where Apple's privacy policy applies.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to deal with your request.

How we use and share information

We use the limited information described above to answer your messages, to keep the website running and secure, and to meet our legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who help us run email and hosting, or where the law requires it.

Our email provider, our web host and Google Fonts may process information outside Canada, including in the United States and Europe, where it is subject to the laws of that country.

How long we keep information

  • App data. It stays on your iPhone until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app erases it from your iPhone. Copies you made yourself stay where you put them: exported backup files, older iPhone backups and anything already written to Apple Health.
  • Emails to us. We keep them for as long as it takes to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Website logs. Kept by our host for a limited time for security, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app. You control your data directly: edit or remove entries in the app, turn permissions off in Settings, or delete the app to erase everything stored by it on your iPhone.
  • With us. Under Canadian privacy law, including Quebec's, you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent. Email yuuvenis@protonmail.com and we will respond within the time the law requires, and in any case within 30 days.
  • If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom. You have the same rights, plus the right to object to our use of your information and to receive it in a portable form. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure website for server logs, and on your request when we answer your emails.
  • If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. You can still ask what we hold and ask us to delete it, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.
  • Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec if you live in Quebec, or your local privacy regulator.

Security

Your app data is protected by the security of your iPhone, including its passcode and encryption. Apple Health keeps its data encrypted while your iPhone is locked. Keep your device locked and up to date, and store any backup file you export somewhere safe.

Children

Myolith is not directed at children, and the app does not collect personal information from them or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so clearly on this page and, where the change affects the app, in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
